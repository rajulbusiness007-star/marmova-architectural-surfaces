# MARMOVA Architectural Surfaces — Luxury Vitrified Porcelain & Slabs Exporter

> A premier, production-grade international tile manufacturer and exporter website featuring luxury dark-mode aesthetics, editorial typography, an interactive WebGL Three.js 3D Room Visualizer, strict physics-based Container Loadability Calculator, Room Quantity & Wastage Planner, and 1-Click Country-Routed WhatsApp RFQ Conversion.

---

## 1. Features & Architecture

- **Visual Brand & Theme**: Dark luxury editorial interface (`#090D16` Obsidian Navy, Champagne Gold `#D4AF37`, Frosted Glass panels, and PP Neue Montreal / Plus Jakarta Sans typography).
- **Interactive 3D Room Visualizer (WebGL / Three.js)**:
  - 5 realistic room presets: Executive Villa Living Lounge, Presidential Spa Bathroom, 5-Star Hotel Lobby, Kitchen Island Suite, and Master Bedroom Retreat.
  - Interactive orbital camera rotation, zoom, auto-orbit, and camera angle reset.
  - Independent surface selection for floors and walls with real-time procedural lighting.
- **Container Loadability Calculator (20ft Dry FCL & 40ft High Cube)**:
  - Physics-based payload weight calculations (max 28,000 kg), pallet dimensions, and ISPM-15 packaging rules.
- **Room Tile Quantity & Wastage Calculator**:
  - Net floor area, wall heights, window deductions, wastage allowance, and box round-up math.
- **One-Click WhatsApp RFQ with Regional Routing**:
  - Automatic routing to international export directors across 9 major global markets.

---

## 2. Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 19, TypeScript
- **Bundler**: Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4, custom glassmorphism and bento utilities
- **Motion**: Motion (`motion/react`)
- **3D Graphics**: Three.js WebGL Engine
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons
